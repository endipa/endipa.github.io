\section{Editorial board}
Decides the scope and the structure of the wiki, appoints the associate editors, and settles a discussion that ends without consensus.
\placeholder{Names and roles.}

\section{Associate editors}
Each part of the wiki has at least one associate editor, who follows the talk pages of its pages and its Zulip channel, runs the discussion of every proposal, asks members of the community for a review when it helps, and decides; accepted changes are written into the wiki by an editor. A busy part or page can have several.
\placeholder{Names, with their parts.}

\section{Community}
Members of the wiki and of the Zulip, who propose, review and discuss changes. Every member can endorse a new member; editors can remove a member whose participation disturbs the work rather than helping it. Early-stage researchers (PhD students, and researchers up to two years after the PhD) can choose to be listed as such.
